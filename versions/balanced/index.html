FAQ

Common questions.

How much does a rooftop solar system cost?

It depends on system size, components and your roof. A typical home system is 2–5 kW, and the PM Surya Ghar subsidy reduces the cost considerably. We give you an itemised quote after a free site survey.

How much subsidy can I get?

Under PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, homes get ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the 3rd kW, up to ₹78,000. We handle the application.

Does solar work during the monsoon?

Yes. Panels generate less on cloudy days, but Hyderabad's strong sun for most of the year keeps annual savings high.

What is net metering?

It lets you export surplus power to the grid and get credit on your bill. We arrange the bi-directional meter and DISCOM approval.

How long does installation take?

Most home installations take a few days once material is on site. Net-meter approval follows DISCOM timelines, and we track it for you.

What maintenance is needed?

Very little: regular panel cleaning and an occasional health check. We offer maintenance plans.
