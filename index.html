How much does a rooftop solar system cost?

It depends on system size, components and your roof. A typical home system is 2–5 kW. After the PM Surya Ghar subsidy, the cost to you drops considerably. We'll give you an exact, itemised quote after a free site survey.

How much subsidy can I get?

Under PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, residential homes can get ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the 3rd kW, up to a maximum of ₹78,000. We handle the application for you.

Will solar work during the monsoon or on cloudy days?

Yes. Panels still generate on cloudy days, just less than in full sun. Hyderabad gets strong sunshine for most of the year, so your annual savings stay high.

What is net metering?

Net metering lets you export extra solar power to the grid and get credit for it on your bill. We arrange the bi-directional meter and DISCOM approvals.

How long does installation take?

Most home installations are finished within a few days of the material reaching your site. Approvals and net-meter installation follow DISCOM timelines, and we track them for you.

What maintenance does a solar system need?

Very little. Clean the panels regularly to remove dust and get an occasional health check. We offer maintenance plans so you never have to think about it.
